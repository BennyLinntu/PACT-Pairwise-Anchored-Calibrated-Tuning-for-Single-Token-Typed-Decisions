"Let PACT play" asks a 9B model fine-tuned for business-document classification to place each Tetris piece, choosing from a short list of legal placements the game already worked out two moves ahead for it — it was never trained to play games, so expect uneven, not masterful, play. Needs python tetris/server.py running locally.
